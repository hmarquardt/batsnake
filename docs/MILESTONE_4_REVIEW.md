# Milestone 4 visual review

Baseline: commit `2c25272`, reviewed 2026-07-31. The sixteen local Milestone 3 captures were used as a single contact-sheet orientation aid; live WebGL2 play at 1440×900 and 1920×1080 supplied the useful baseline. Fixed seeds were exercised in both modes and all quality profiles. Many software-rendered captures are too dark to judge surface detail, so findings below favor problems reproduced in live play.

| # | Moment and intended read | Baseline communication | Correction, cost, and fallback |
|---:|---|---|---|
| 1 | Menu, mode choice, and field report should frame one coherent natural-history observation. | Typography is strong, but the cave behind it is muddy, glare dominates some angles, and the report is visually detached from the encounter. | Improve world composition first; retain the restrained UI and seed/report structure. No gameplay or render-target cost. |
| 2 | First flap, glide, brake, dive, panic, and capture should read through peripheral anatomy and effort. | The pilot bat reads as small wedges; state changes and the fallback are difficult to see. | Replace it with the project-created skinned hero bat, cross-fade six states, keep torso below the reticle, and retain a complete procedural rig. Cost: 70,824-byte GLB, 782 triangles, 15 bones, one mixer, no textures. |
| 3 | Quick echo should sketch nearby structure; deep echo should establish distance; a close boa should feel organic and alarming. | Repetitive bands and fine speckle compete with feature order, while snake returns lack body mass. | Use broad world-space response, reduce fine surface contribution, soften organic returns, and preserve bounded histories. No new render targets; procedural snake echo remains complete. Some travel-front banding on faceted walls remains visible. |
| 4 | Dense emergence and panic should show layered routes, air displacement, and faster evasive wing rhythm. | Traffic reads as small repeated silhouettes and panic is much clearer in telemetry than in motion. | Retain the instanced flock, improve wing timing/heat regions, and consume panic state visually. Seven fixed instanced batches avoid per-bat mixers; quality no longer changes simulated flock count. |
| 5 | Normal snake vision should show a heavy animal; preparation should show stillness, an S-curve, compression, jaw readiness, and three distinct posts. | Tubes, balloon-like first-person cheeks, and weak darkness silhouettes make the boa feel provisional. | Use one bounded continuously deformed 560-vertex body per snake, shaped head/jaw/pits/tongue, breathing, identity color/size, and post-specific recovery motion. Controller and strike path remain unchanged; the whole animal is procedural and cannot fail to load. |
| 6 | Lunge, contact, capture, miss, recoil, and recovery should communicate physical cause without HUD text. | Contact is small, miss types look alike, and generic motion carries too much of the event. | Add visual muscle flow, jaw contact, a settling captured bat, reason-specific recovery, event-tied audio/camera/haptics, and reduced-sensory alternatives. Presentation consumes the existing strike result and does not alter collision or timing. |
| 7 | Broad thermal should preserve cool cave depth and warm anatomy; focus should clarify motion rather than vignette it. | Bats can disappear, companion snakes flatten into red shapes, and focus changes the label more than motion readability. | Separate head/torso/root/membrane heat, retain occlusion, lengthen central trails under focus, and bound trails at 12/48/112. No new thermal target; low remains tactically complete. |
| 8 | The cave mouth and ambush zones should be recognizable landmarks without blocking flight lanes. | Repeated faceted primitives, uniform wetness, flat exterior light, and duplicated formations reveal construction. | Add deterministic erosion, wet/dry and mineral/guano breakup, batched formation profiles, entrance haze/vegetation, and post identities. Data textures and instancing keep runtime local and bounded; collision/navigation geometry stays authoritative. |
| 9 | Snake switching and all camera/tactile events should feel embodied but remain accessible. | Switching is mostly a camera jump; collision/capture feedback is generic or absent. | Preserve the awareness transfer, add physical-event impulses and optional dual-rumble, attenuate all new feedback for reduced-motion/flashing, and fail silently without Gamepad vibration. |

Implementation priority followed visible leverage: production bat and fallback; cave lighting/surface batching; boa/contact presentation; sensory/audio/tactile cleanup; then release validation and captures. The principal remaining visual weakness is echo travel-front banding across nearby faceted cave surfaces.
