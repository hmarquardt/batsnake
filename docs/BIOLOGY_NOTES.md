# Biology notes

## Scope

Batsnake is a first-person wildlife *simulation game* — not a biology simulator. The following notes document where the game abstracts, simplifies, or speculates about real Cuban boas and cave bats. These are not corrections of the game but clarifications for anyone reading biological claims into game mechanics.

## Cuban boas (Chilabrothrus angulifer)

### Heat-sensitive facial pits

- **Documented**: Cuban boas, like many boids, have labial pits that detect infrared radiation. These are less studied than pit-viper loreal pits but are accepted as functional thermoreceptors.
- **Simplified for play**: The game's instant wide-area thermal perception, color-mapped persistence trails, and focus vignette are dramatic translations. Real boa thermal resolution, field of view, update rate, and brain integration time are unknown and almost certainly much slower, narrower, and less colorful.

### Bat hunting

- **Documented**: Cuban boas have been observed hanging from cave ceilings and striking at passing bats. Coordination between multiple boas at a single flight line is not well-documented but plausible.
- **Simplified for play**: The shared tactical awareness, route memory, strike learning, and companion coordination are game abstractions. Real boas may hunt independently near each other without the responsive, memory-driven cooperation the game suggests.

### Strike mechanics

- **Inspired by**: Boas strike forward with an open mouth, seize prey, and constrict. The game models charge, lunge, swept collision, capture, and recovery.
- **Simplified for play**: The exaggerated S-curve body compression, thermal head tension, and jaw gape are readability aids. Real strikes are faster, shorter-range, and resolved by contact rather than swept collision volumes.

## Bats

### Echolocation

- **Inspired by**: Cave bats emit ultrasonic calls and interpret returning echoes to navigate and hunt.
- **Simplified for play**: The game translates ultrasonic frequencies into audible chirps, visual shell pulses, and geometric surface reconstruction. Real bat echolocation is ultrasonic, processed by specialized auditory cortex over milliseconds, and does not produce visible luminous geometry. The separation into "quick" and "deep" call modes is a gameplay mechanic, not a documented bat behavior.

### Group positioning and emergence

- **Documented**: Bat emergences from cave roosts have pulses, gaps, route preferences, and collective reactions to threats.
- **Simplified for play**: The encounter director, five authored routes, behavioral profiles (leader, follower, edge, juvenile, escape), and panic propagation are compressed into a short gameplay session. Real emergence events involve thousands of bats over longer periods with more complex social dynamics.

### Flight model

- **Inspired by**: Bat flight involves wingbeats, gliding, momentum, and maneuverability.
- **Simplified for play**: The game's momentum-based flight with speed-dependent turning, stall, dive, brake, and energy regeneration is a readable game control system. Real bat flight physics are far more complex, involving flexible membranous wings with active camber control.

## Cave environment

- **Inspired by**: Tropical Cuban limestone caves with dissolved chambers, fractures, stalactites/stalagmites, columns, flowstone, draperies, breakdown rubble, guano deposits, humid entrances, and clustered bat roosts.
- **Environmental logic**: The authored cave places guano below the main colony, wet flowstone and drips along a few seep paths, algae and plant matter near the mouth, and boa supports beside flight corridors. These relationships are plausible composition rules, not documentation of one surveyed cave.
- **Simplified for play**: The cave is a fictional authored chamber, not a scan or reconstruction of a named Cuban site. The 96 low-detail resting silhouettes represent a much larger colony; their depletion is synchronized to the compressed encounter director. Exterior flora, water frequency, mineral color, acoustic returns, thermal contrast, and named landmarks are artistic treatments.

## General disclaimer

The game is designed for ecological *evocation*, not biological *instruction*. Where the game presents animal behavior as settled fact, it does so in the service of readable gameplay. Players interested in real Cuban boa and bat biology should consult primary literature and field guides rather than extrapolating from this game.
